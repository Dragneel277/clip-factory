Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Clip Factory ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • Through Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API, the app accesses only the YouTube channels the owner signs in with, in order to upload videos, set video metadata, thumbnails and playlists, and read basic statistics of those channels.
  • The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How data is stored

  • OAuth tokens and video files are stored only on the owner's private server, with access restricted to the owner.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when a channel is disconnected, and any stored data can be deleted on request.
